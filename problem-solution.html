Problem and Solution

The Problem

Like many students and young professionals, I’ve constantly been told how crucial networking is for career development. Research shows that developing strong professional networks directly improves career success and employment opportunities. However, taking those first steps into professional networking is daunting. Questions like "How do I interact with recruiters?", "How should I present myself?", and "Where do I even find these opportunities?" can feel overwhelming to navigate as a first-year student.

The Solution

Build a Bridge was created to bridge the gap for young professionals navigating career development and building meaningful connections. It serves as a one-stop resource for learning how to network with tools like a networking event calendar, outreach templates, guides for navigating events and conversations, and more to help university students take their first steps into their careers with confidence.
