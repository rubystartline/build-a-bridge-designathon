EMAIL

Cold Email to Alumni / Industry Professional

A high-conversion initial outreach message asking for a 15-minute informal coffee chat.

Subject: Quick question from a [Your University] student interested in [Their Field/Company]

Hi [Name],

I came across your profile while researching [Industry/Company Name] and was really inspired by your work on [Specific Project or Achievement].

I’m currently a [Year] studying [Major] at [University]. As someone looking to break into [Field], I’d love to learn more about your journey and any advice you might have.

Would you be open to a quick 15-minute virtual coffee chat sometime in the next few weeks?

Best regards,
[Your Name]
